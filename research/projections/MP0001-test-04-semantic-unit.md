# MP0001 Test 04 — Semantic unit reduction

status: experiment
contract: MP0001
source_evidence: E0002-matome-incompleteness-useful.md

## Reduction targets

S1: Incomplete Matome was useful.

S2: Incomplete → useful.

S3: useful.

S4: incomplete.

## Observation

S1 preserves the explicit relation while retaining both semantic poles.

S2 preserves the relation in a more abstract form, but removes the explicit subject and temporal/contextual framing.

S3 and S4 preserve only one pole and therefore cannot represent the observed relation by themselves.

## Result

For this tested pair, the smallest demonstrated semantic unit that still expresses the observed change is a relation with at least two distinguishable states/poles and a directional or associative relation between them.

The subject, time, and conditions may be recoverable from external lineage/context, but cannot be assumed to be irrelevant in general.

## Non-claim

This does not establish a universal semantic grammar for Matome. It only identifies a candidate minimum relation structure for this experiment.

## Next test

Apply the same reduction to a Matome whose meaning depends on subject, time, or condition, and test whether those fields become part of the minimum semantic unit.
