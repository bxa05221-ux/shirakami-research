# CE0001 — Counter-evidence test: lineage removal

status: tested
parent_question: Q0001
source_evidence: E0002-matome-incompleteness-useful.md

## Counter-hypothesis

Lineage may not be necessary for a useful Matome representation. A compact representation containing only the observed statement might be sufficient for some uses.

## Test

Compare the full tested representation against a representation containing only:

> Matome was incomplete and useful.

## Result

The reduced representation remains readable and may be useful as a human reminder, but it can no longer answer:

- which Evidence supports the statement;
- which Delta produced it;
- what remains unresolved;
- which claims were explicitly excluded.

## Counter-evidence assessment

The test does not refute the need for lineage when Matome is used as part of a verifiable Landscape system.

It does show that lineage is not required for every possible human-readable summary.

## Conclusion

Lineage is therefore a **system integrity property**, not necessarily a requirement of every surface representation.

This weakens the stronger claim "all Matome must contain lineage" and supports the narrower claim "the system must preserve lineage outside or alongside the surface representation."

## Next question

Can lineage be maintained by the runtime/metadata layer while allowing extremely small Matome surface representations?
