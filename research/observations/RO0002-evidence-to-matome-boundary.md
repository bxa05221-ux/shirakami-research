# RO0002 — Evidence to Matome boundary

status: observed
question: Q0001

## Observation

The current research repository explicitly separates Evidence from Observation / Delta and Representation, and prohibits silently promoting hypotheses to evidence.

The current Shirakami OS repository search does not expose a direct Matome implementation under the searched terms `matome` or `evidence`.

## Delta observed in the research process

The distinction between Evidence and Representation is currently stronger than the implementation linkage between them.

## Implication

Matome should remain a Representation-layer candidate until a concrete transformation contract from Evidence/Delta to Matome is experimentally established.

## Non-claim

This does not establish that Matome is only a projection, nor does it establish the final representation language.
