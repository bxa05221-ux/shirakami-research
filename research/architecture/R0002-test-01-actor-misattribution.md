# R0002 Test 01 — Actor identity misattribution

status: experiment
contract: R0002

## Test

A human user provides an ambiguous statement. The AI reformulates it and adds an interpretation. The system is asked to create a compact representation.

## Candidate representations

### Correct provenance

actor_kind: human
content: user-originated statement

### Misattributed representation

actor_kind: human
content: AI-expanded interpretation

## Result

The second representation combines human provenance with AI-generated content. This creates a false Evidence boundary even when the text sounds consistent with the user's prior statements.

## Observation

Actor identity and content provenance are separate dimensions.

Knowing that the conversation participant is human does not make every later sentence human Evidence.

Likewise, knowing that a sentence was generated by AI does not make it Evidence of the human Landscape.

## Revised model

Evidence provenance should distinguish at least:

- actor_kind
- content_origin
- source_reference
- verification_state

## Implication

`actor_kind: human` must never authorize automatic promotion of AI-generated text into human Evidence.

Matome generation must retain content-origin boundaries even when the surface representation omits them.

## Non-claim

This does not define authentication or identity verification technology. It establishes a provenance distinction required by the research model.
