# D0002 — Dialogue to Evidence cycle

status: experimental

## Cycle

Human Evidence
→ Dialogue
→ Observed Delta
→ Matome Candidate
→ AI Hypothesis
→ Human Revision
→ New Observed Delta
→ Verification
→ Evidence

## Rules

1. Human-originated statements remain distinguishable from AI-generated hypotheses.
2. A Matome Candidate is a derived representation, not Evidence by generation.
3. AI Hypothesis may propose relations or questions but does not expand Evidence scope silently.
4. Human Revision is observable only through a new user-provided statement or state change.
5. A new Evidence record requires verification of the observed transition.
6. Previous Evidence remains immutable.

## Purpose

Test whether ordinary AI conversation can be modeled as a measurable change process in the user's Landscape without treating the AI's interpretation as human Evidence.

## Relation to Matome

Matome functions as a temporary compression surface between observed Delta and the next conversational transition. Its representation language is not fixed by this experiment.

## Open questions

- What constitutes a measurable Human Revision?
- Can multiple Matome representations preserve the same transition identity?
- How should unresolved hypotheses be linked without becoming Evidence?
